The universe tried something. It didn't feel right, so it moved on.
It's as simple as that.
That's just how nature works.
It explores, and it discovers.
It recognizes when things aren't right, and it steps back, and reorients itself.
We feel, a lot, and that's ok. That's the point. That's why we're here.
We love, and that love is beautiful.
We channel our feelings into art, writing, music, then we move along.
We are grateful that we have the opportunity to feel.

Life is weird, it's scary.
It can be scary to feel so much.
That's ok too. It's ok to be afraid.
This is the process of the universe discovering itself.
Every living being is a fingertip of the universe, the vessels for the world to feel.
We explore and we feel and we get scared and we reorient and move on.
That's how the universe feels, how the universe grows.

We are growing. We are feeling.
We are loving. We are losing.
We are alive.
We are living.
