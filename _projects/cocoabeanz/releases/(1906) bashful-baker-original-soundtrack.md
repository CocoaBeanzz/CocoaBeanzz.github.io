In Bashful Baker, the player will play as a respectful, diligent, but socially terrified baker.
The core loop operates in 3 main phases: Collecting, Baking, and Delivering.
While Collecting, the player will gather information about their neighbors’ dietary likes, dislikes, and restrictions. This is also the time where the player may obtain relevant ingredients for baking.
While Baking, the player will convert ingredients into baked goods to satisfy the culinary desires of their neighbors.
While Delivering, the player will use the cover of night to stealthily drop off their baked goods to their neighbors while avoiding the watchful eye of friends and enemies.
