The page's own HTML is the whole body (no .prose column, no related posts), styled by _sass/_product.scss, with the little behavior it has in assets/js/product.js (the voice switch, counters, scroll reveals). Front matter a product page can set: layout: product name: Kouncil download_url: https://... # the Get button; without it, the page says the product is coming price: "$49" # shown on the Get button when set platforms: Windows and macOS {%- endcomment -%} {{ content }}
