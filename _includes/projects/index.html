{%- comment -%} Search box, label filters and card grid for the current department's projects. `section_projects` is every project whose folder is _projects//, plus projects from other departments that list this one under `also_in` (see _plugins/sections.rb). A project with `unlisted: true` in its front matter is left out of the listing. It keeps its page and can still be featured on the landing. A listing page with `square_tiles: true` in its front matter crops tile images square (for album artwork) instead of wide.
